about me

The developer behind SMT.

20 years old. 5 years of hands-on building. Still shipping.

Hi, I'm the person behind the SMT ecosystem.

I started writing code at 15, and five years in, I'm still doing the same thing that got me hooked in the first place — taking an idea and turning it into something people can actually open and use. No formal degree, no bootcamp certificate on the wall. Just a few thousand hours of trial, error, and shipped products.

My work lives mostly in the PHP and JavaScript world — full-stack builds that run on ordinary shared hosting, no bloated frameworks required. Under the SMT name, I've built hosting platforms, growth tools, browser utilities, and a keyboard app, each one solving a real, specific problem instead of chasing trends.

I care about two things above everything else: does it work, and is it clean. If a feature doesn't earn its place, it doesn't ship.
